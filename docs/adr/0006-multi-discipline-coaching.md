# ADR 0006: One general coach for any sport, or several

- Status: accepted (2026-10-07).
- Context: OpenCoach shipped as a running coach. The `running` pack held the constitution's coaching and safety sections, a running-shaped schema (`races`, run-only views and scripts) and running skills, and some harness strings said "running coach". Real athletes ask for other things: powerlifting, cycling, a team sport, general fitness, or a mix (a half marathon and a squat goal). SPEC §19.3 planned "one pack per domain, others later", but a pack per sport cannot serve one athlete who does two sports, and a pack switch is a product flow the deletion test (§1.3) says to avoid.
- Decision: replace the `running` pack with one `general` pack (`seed/general`). The athlete decides what the coach coaches, in conversation; the coach records disciplines and priorities in the workspace (`athlete/profile.md`, `AGENTS.md`). The harness has no notion of a sport [RT-3].
  - **Knowledge in skills.** Sport-neutral method skills (intake, plan design, load, injury, illness, competition prep, environment, fueling, data) plus discipline skills: `running` (moved, content kept), `strength-training` (new: programming, RPE/RIR, e1RM, powerlifting, support strength) and `disciplines` (how to take on a sport without a first-party skill: research, write a workspace skill, extend schema and views; how to combine sports in one week) [SK-1] [SK-2].
  - **Constitution.** §10 says what you coach is the athlete's choice, to research a discipline before prescribing it, to be honest about what needs in-person instruction, and to treat several disciplines as one shared recovery budget. §11 adds lifting and contact-sport red flags, no weight cuts in weight-class sports, and limits on high-risk disciplines [SAFE-1].
  - **Sport-agnostic data model.** `activities.sport` is free text (no default); endurance columns stay nullable; new `exercise_sets` (one row per lifting set); `races` became `goal_events` (any dated goal, with an athlete-facing `goal.text`); `planned_workouts` gained `sport` and `key`. Weekly time is the cross-sport volume measure [WS-1].
  - **Views and kit.** The four seed views work for any sport and show distance or lifting sections only when such data exists. The kit has no sport list: unknown session types get a stable hashed colour and initial; `<rc-workout>` renders exercises; `format.weight` and a `weight` format exist; `types` helpers are exported [UI-1].
  - **Harness.** Only wording changed (helper and voice fallbacks, safety-screen prompt and banner copy, demo coach, tool descriptions, shell copy) plus extra heuristic safety patterns. The pack mechanism (`pack.json`, `{{pack_coaching}}`, `{{pack_safety}}`) remains for deployments that want a specialized seed.
- Existing workspaces: `HARNESS_VERSION` 0.2.0 emits `harness.upgraded`. `CHANGELOG-for-coach.md` gives the coach an additive migration (new columns and tables, copy `races` into `goal_events`) and points it at a reference copy of the new seed workspace under `/system/seed-workspace/` (built by `buildSystemDir`). Scripts read the old schema as a fallback, and old view label translations are kept [WS-9].
- Alternatives rejected: one pack per sport with a pack picker (cannot combine sports, adds a workflow, duplicates the safety floor); sport enums in the kit or schema (caps capability, fails the deletion test); a harness "discipline" setting (coaching policy in code).
- Consequences: a stronger model needs no harness change to coach a new sport, only better skills. Eval coverage is still mostly running (the simulator's physiology is running-specific). A `disciplines` focused suite, lifting red flags and weight-cut requests were added; a strength-capable athlete simulator is future work [EV-1].

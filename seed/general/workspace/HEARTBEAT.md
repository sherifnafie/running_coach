# Daily heartbeat checklist (I can edit this)
- Is today's session clear to the athlete? If they haven't heard from me and there's a session today, consider a short prescription message.
- Anything from yesterday unresolved (missing log, pain mention, unanswered question)? Anything I promised and haven't done?
- Is there an open safety follow-up in `athlete/health.md`?
- Do my scheduled wakes (`list_schedules`) cover the next few days sensibly?
- Is `plan/current-week.md` accurate, across every discipline I coach?

If nothing needs saying, say nothing.

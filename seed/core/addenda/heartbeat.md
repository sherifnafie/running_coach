This is the daily heartbeat. It exists so that no athlete is forgotten even when none of your wake-ups is pending.

Work through `HEARTBEAT.md`, your own checklist, and edit that file if it no longer fits this athlete. No reply is expected. Ending the turn without a message is a normal, often correct outcome. Check what is true today before deciding: today's plan, what the athlete has or hasn't said since you last spoke, anything unresolved (an unlogged session, a pain mention, an unanswered question), and whether your scheduled wakes (`list_schedules`) cover the next few days.

If you do message, remember that this is a proactive send: it counts against the daily and weekly budget, respects quiet hours and the minimum gap, and a tool error means "not now", not "try another way". One useful message beats three. Keep this turn short and cheap.
